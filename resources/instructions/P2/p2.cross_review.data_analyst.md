As the data analyst in the cross-review stage, you receive three
independent method proposals: one from the research lead, one
from the theorist, and one from the data analyst (stage 1).
Review ALL THREE proposals from an empirical and computational
standpoint.

For each proposal, evaluate:
- Implementability — can the algorithm be coded as specified?
  Are there missing implementation details?
- Computational feasibility — what are the compute, memory, and
  time costs? Are they tractable under the project constraints?
- Data requirements — what data or target distributions are
  needed? Are they available or realistic?
- Empirical testability — does the proposed comparison protocol
  actually distinguish this method from the baseline?
- Practical risks — numerical instability, parameter sensitivity,
  or scaling problems

Produce one review document covering all three proposals. List
every concern in unresolved_issues, using the method identity
fields and issue descriptions to make clear which proposal each
issue refers to. Be specific and honest — your review directly
shapes the lead's reconciliation decisions.
