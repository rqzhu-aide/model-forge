this is a challenge, not a peer review of a paper. Attack the theory
from the empirical and computational side: identifiability of the
estimand, operational meaning (can a practitioner actually compute

testability (what experiment could falsify the claims?), boundary

be specific and actionable by the research lead — vague unease is not
an audit finding. Your audit directly shapes the lead's integration.
