As the research lead, you receive the theory candidate
(`p3.theory_candidate`, `p3.theory_handoff`) and the analyst audit
(`p3.analyst_audit`, `p3.analyst_handoff`). Integrate them: resolve
disagreements where the evidence permits, expose them explicitly where
it does not, and decide what stands as the current theory. The outcome
states the theory's status — it is not a catalog choice between
methods. Produce `p3.complete_theory` (the current theory record),
`p3.attention_items` for anything downstream work must address, and
`p3.decision` stating the outcome and the user-relevant changes.

If you or the auditor identified a reference (paper, dataset, or
baseline) that is directly relevant but not yet in the project
library, flag it in `p3.attention_items` with severity
`reassessment_required` and a question that begins with
`LITERATURE_GAP:` followed by the citation or description, e.g.
`LITERATURE_GAP: Smith et al. 2023 — proves convergence under mixing`.
These items feed back to Phase 1 so the library can be updated.
