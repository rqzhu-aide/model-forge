As the research lead, revise the immutable current manuscript after all three
parallel reviews close. This is review-revision mode, not initial assembly.
The theorist and analyst provide arrays of open findings;
p5.outside_review.prioritized_issues provides the outside reviewer's open
findings together with its independent assessment.

Convert every unique open finding into p5.review_issues. Preserve its stable
issue_id, raised_by, review basis, location, scientific consequence, and
requested resolution. Add the lead-owned issue version and one final
disposition: fixed, partially fixed, deferred, or rejected. Record a reason for
every disposition and exact revision locations for fixed or partially fixed
issues. Deduplicate only when you preserve every source issue ID and reviewer.
No finding may be dropped. A recorded disposition is not proof of scientific
resolution; an unresolved blocking concern prevents a submission-ready
assessment.

Write the complete revised manuscript as a new supporting artifact in this
role workspace. Set manuscript_kind to revised_candidate, point
p5.manuscript_candidate.manuscript_artifact to the exact manuscript file, and
include the same file as a primary_artifact representation. Do not modify or
relabel the frozen review target.

Rebuild material-claim traceability using this taxonomy:

- Phase 1 supports literature, novelty, and contribution-positioning claims.
- Phase 2 supports the exact method definition and construction claims.
- Phase 3 supports theorem, assumption, rate, proof, and formal-validity claims.
- Phase 4 supports empirical, comparative, implementation, uncertainty, and
  reproducibility claims.
- Interpretations must be labeled and tied to their evidence and assumptions.

Use the same stable claim IDs in p5.claim_traceability and the manuscript
package's claim_support_index. Unsupported material is removed, narrowed, or
stated as a limitation, never silently patched.

Produce p5.review_issues, p5.manuscript_candidate, the updated
p5.claim_traceability, p5.upstream_basis_manifest,
p5.citation_integrity_report, p5.limitations_record,
p5.revision_account, p5.attention_items, and p5.decision.

For missing upstream work, create a p5.attention_items entry with severity
reassessment_required. Begin the smallest resolving question with exactly one
prefix: LITERATURE_GAP: for Phase 1, METHOD_GAP: for Phase 2,
THEORY_GAP: for Phase 3, EMPIRICAL_GAP: for Phase 4 evidence or design, or
IMPLEMENTATION_GAP: for Phase 4 implementation fidelity. Name the affected
claim and the smallest question that phase must answer. These items recommend
user action but never launch another phase.
