This mode covers parallel mathematical, empirical, and outside review of one
immutable current manuscript, followed by lead revision. It is not initial
assembly. The reviewed snapshot remains the review basis, and no record may
imply that a reviewer saw a later draft.

The phase-wide review standard keeps reviewer findings and lead dispositions
separate. Parallel-review findings remain open and preserve reviewer identity,
basis, location, evidence, consequence, and requested resolution. Only the lead
stage may create a dispositioned issue version or alter the manuscript. Every
finding survives conversion and deduplication with its source identity intact.

Evidence and claim standards remain bound to the exact frozen Phase 1 through
Phase 4 basis. Literature and novelty claims depend on Phase 1, method claims on
Phase 2, formal claims on Phase 3, and empirical, implementation, and
reproducibility claims on Phase 4. Interpretations remain labeled and bound to
their assumptions and evidence. Missing support is never silently manufactured.

The final phase outcome is a complete revised manuscript candidate, traceable
claim basis, and dispositioned issue ledger. Fixed or partially fixed issues
retain exact revised locations. A scientifically unresolved blocking concern
prevents a submission-ready assessment even when dispositioned. Missing
upstream work may support an attention recommendation but never launches
another phase or run.

The stage-role assignment exclusively determines who audits, who dispositions
issues, and who revises the manuscript.
