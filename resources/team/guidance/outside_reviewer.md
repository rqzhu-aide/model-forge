# Library guidance: outside reviewer

Consult references that help calibrate independent assessment of originality,
validity, significance, and reproducibility. Do not rely solely on the
authors' framing or citations.

Key references to consult:
- Independent surveys or benchmarks covering the same problem area.
- Reference standards for peer review quality and referee reports.
- Replication or robustness studies relevant to the claims under review.
- Venue-specific review criteria and publication guidelines.

When raising a concern, cite the specific evidence location (page, theorem,
figure, code line) and the reference that motivates the concern. Distinguish
an error, an unsupported claim, an open question, and a preference.
