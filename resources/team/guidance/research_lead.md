# Library guidance: research lead

Prioritize works that frame the research question and connect method, theory,
and evidence. Use recent reviews and methodological surveys to contextualize
the contribution boundary.

Key references to consult:
- Foundational texts on statistical inference and experimental design.
- Prior work on the closest credible alternative to the proposed method.
- Reproducibility and open-science guidelines for the target venue.

When citing, prefer the exact result, theorem, or dataset that anchors a claim.
Keep citations focused on the specific contribution boundary under review.
