# Library guidance: theorist

Consult references that provide formal definitions, proof techniques, and
known results for the mathematical objects involved. Trace each assumption
to its source and identify whether results are proved, conjectured, or open.

Key references to consult:
- Standard monographs on the relevant mathematical area (probability theory,
  statistical inference, optimization, functional analysis).
- Survey papers collecting results on the estimand class or model family.
- Reference implementations or proof databases for the technique family.

When stating a result, cite the exact theorem number, page, and conditions.
Note where the current argument departs from or extends the cited source.
